# Development OutputDocs

Finished development-phase deliverables. See [Development](../README.md) for this phase and the [pipeline README](../../README.md) for the shared rules.

## What to put here

Completed specs, stories, and plans that QA and implementers will consume. These are documents, not application code.

QA is the next reader. It copies what it needs into [QA InputDocs](../../3_QA/3-1_InputDocs/README.md). Those copies are then frozen.

## What not to do

- Do not put drafts, memlogs, or other scratch here. Those stay in [2-3_Working](../2-3_Working/README.md).
- Do not edit these files from QA. QA copies them.
- Do not revise a file here in place after QA has copied it. A new finished version is a new file, or a deliberate replacement by the developer before downstream copies exist.

## Where the workflow reads and writes

The workflow reads [2-1_InputDocs](../2-1_InputDocs/README.md) and [2-2_ProcessedDocs](../2-2_ProcessedDocs/README.md), runs in [2-3_Working](../2-3_Working/README.md), and writes the finished result here.
